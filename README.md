Circle Search Auto-Clicker

A project written in C# designed for screens with a resolution of 1920x1080 (100% scaling).

How It Works:
The bot captures screenshots of the screen, searching for circles of a configurable diameter. Once a circle is detected, the bot automatically moves the cursor to its location and right-clicks on it.

How to Use:
Activate the bot by pressing the F key.
To deactivate the bot, press the C key.
Once activated, the bot will continuously search for circles and interact with them as described.
